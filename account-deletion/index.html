Delete your KidoSteps account and data

Version 1.0 · Effective and last updated: 30 July 2026

Delete in the app

Open KidoSteps → Settings → Account settings → Delete account and confirm the deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email kidosteps.support@gmail.com from the address used for your account.

Do not send passwords, child photos or health records by email.

Requests are normally handled within one month.

What happens when you delete your account

  • Your account and all associated personal data are permanently deleted.
  • If you are the last active adult in a family, all related child data and records are also deleted.
  • If another adult remains, shared family data remains accessible to that family.
  • Images stored in Cloudflare R2 are deleted after the database deletion completes.
  • Some data may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until they are automatically overwritten.
  • Local data on your device is cleared when the app signs out. Uninstall the app from other devices if needed.

Google Play subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel your Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions.

Delete specific data

You can delete individual child profiles and records directly in the app. For other requests, contact support.
