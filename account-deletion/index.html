Delete your KidoSteps account and data

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Delete in the app

Open KidoSteps → Settings → Account settings → Delete account and confirm the deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email kidosteps.support@gmail.com from the address used for your KidoSteps account. Use the subject “KidoSteps account deletion request”. We may ask for limited additional information to verify that the account belongs to you.

Do not send a password, child photos, health records or other sensitive information by email.

We will acknowledge the request and act without undue delay, normally within one month. We will explain any lawful extension or any information that must temporarily be retained.

What happens

  • Your sign-in access is disabled and direct account identifiers are deleted or irreversibly anonymised.
  • If you are the last active adult in the family, the family's child profiles, care logs, notes, activities and stored images are deleted.
  • If another active adult remains, shared child and family data remains available to that family. Delete individual child records first, or state in your support request that you are also requesting deletion of child data you are authorised to manage.
  • Data may remain in encrypted backups until the documented backup cycle expires and may be retained where law strictly requires it. The final published page must state the verified backup period.
  • Local data on a device is cleared when the deletion succeeds and the app signs out. Uninstall KidoSteps from other devices to remove any remaining app storage there.

Google Play subscription

Deleting KidoSteps does not by itself cancel a Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in Google Play subscriptions. Cancellation and refund rights remain subject to applicable law and Google Play's terms.

Deleting selected data only

You can delete individual records and child profiles in the app where that option is available. For another access, correction, restriction, portability or deletion request, email support using the address above.
