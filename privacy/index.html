Privacy Policy – KidoSteps

Effective date: 2026-07-25

This privacy policy applies to the KidoSteps mobile application and related services operated by KidoSteps (the "Service Provider").

Who This Application Is For

KidoSteps is designed exclusively for parents and guardians of infants and young children. The Application is intended for use by adults (18 years or older) and is not intended for direct use by children.

Information Collection and Use
  • Account data: Email address and encrypted password
  • Child data: Name, date of birth, feeding logs, sleep data, growth measurements, and vaccination records
  • Technical data: IP address and device information for security and functionality

The Application does not use tracking technologies, profiling, or automated decision-making related to children.

This data is used solely to provide app functionality. No personal data is sold or used for advertising purposes.

Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal data on the following legal bases under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Contract: to provide the core functionality of the Application that you have signed up for
  • Consent: for data about children, which you voluntarily provide and can withdraw at any time by deleting the relevant data
  • Legitimate interest: for security, fraud prevention, and technical operation of the Application (e.g. IP logging)
Data About Children

Parents and guardians voluntarily provide data about their children in order to use the Application's features. This data:

  • Is entered and controlled entirely by the parent or guardian
  • Is not shared with third parties for commercial purposes
  • Is used only to provide the Application's functionality
  • Can be deleted at any time

The Application does not knowingly collect data directly from children. All child-related data is entered by an adult parent or guardian on the child's behalf.

Parental Responsibility

All data relating to children must be entered and managed by a parent or legal guardian. By using the Application, you confirm that you have the legal right to provide and manage this data.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

If you believe we are processing your personal data unlawfully, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Sweden, this is the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY), www.imy.se. Residents of other countries may lodge a complaint with their local data protection authority.

Data Control and Deletion

Parents and guardians have full control over their child's data.

  • Account and child data can be deleted at any time free of charge
  • Data export functionality may be available as part of premium features
Sub-processors and Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service providers to deliver the Application's functionality. These providers process data on our behalf under data processing agreements and are contractually bound to protect your data:

  • Render – backend hosting and server infrastructure
  • Neon – database hosting (PostgreSQL)
  • Brevo – transactional email delivery (e.g. password reset, account activation, invitations)
  • Google Play Services
Data Storage Location

Your data may be stored and processed on secure servers located both within and outside the European Economic Area (EEA), including in the United States. When data is transferred outside the EEA, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) are used to ensure your data remains protected.

Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the Application's services and up to 12 months after account inactivity, unless otherwise required by law.

Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Passwords are securely encrypted and never stored in plain text.

No Medical Advice

The Application is intended for informational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in our Changelog. Continued use of the Application after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
