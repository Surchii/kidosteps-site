Privacy notice – KidoSteps

Version 1.0 · Effective and last updated: 28 July 2026

This notice explains how personal data is handled when an adult uses the KidoSteps application and related services. KidoSteps is for parents and legal guardians aged 18 or over; it is not offered for children to use directly.

Key points at a glance
  • Only adults aged 18 or over may use KidoSteps; children do not use the app directly.
  • Child data, including health-related information (growth, vaccinations, sleep and feeding), is entered and managed by the guardian and processed based on the guardian's consent.
  • We never sell personal data and do not show advertising.
  • You can delete any child profile, record or your entire account at any time.
  • Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

2. Personal data we process

Adult account and family data

  • Name, email address, date of birth if supplied, emoji/avatar, family membership and role.
  • Invitations and the email addresses of adults invited to a family.
  • Account activation, email-verification and password-reset records. Passwords are stored as salted one-way hashes; KidoSteps does not store the password in readable form.
  • Which family member created or updated a shared record.

Child and care data supplied by an adult

  • Child name, date of birth, emoji, profile picture and gallery photos.
  • Feeding, sleep, diaper, growth, vaccination and milestone records.
  • Notes, planned activities, reminder settings and information entered in free-text fields.
  • Trends and informational warnings calculated from records, such as feeding or sleep intervals.

Subscription data

Subscription type, product identifier, expiry time and a Google Play purchase token used to verify entitlement. Google Play processes payment credentials; KidoSteps does not receive a full payment-card number.

Technical and locally stored data

  • IP address and basic request/security logs processed by the API host, including for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
  • Basic platform/device information used to configure app behaviour and notifications.
  • A login token in secure device storage, plus preferences and selected cached/offline child and care data on the device.
  • Local notification schedules. KidoSteps does not use an advertising identifier for behavioural advertising.

Cookies and tracking

The KidoSteps website does not set cookies for analytics or advertising. The app does not use an advertising identifier or third-party behavioural tracking. A login token is stored in secure device storage to keep the session active; it is removed on sign-out or account deletion.

Please do not enter information that is unnecessary for the feature, especially in free-text notes.

4. Children's data and health-related information

Children merit special protection. Only a parent or legal guardian authorised to act for the child may create a child profile or provide the child's data. The adult must also consider the child's views and evolving capacity where appropriate.

KidoSteps is intended for adults aged 18 or over and is not designed for direct use by children.

KidoSteps relies on the guardian's consent when creating and managing child profiles and entering child-related data. The guardian is responsible for ensuring that they are authorised to provide the child's data.

The adult user is responsible for ensuring that the data entered is appropriate, necessary, and lawful.

Consent may be withdrawn at any time by:

  • Deleting the child profile in the app (Settings → Child → Delete).
  • Deleting individual records linked to the child.
  • Deleting the adult account (Settings → Account → Delete account).
  • Contacting us at kidosteps.support@gmail.com.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. Features that depend on the deleted data will stop working.

Invited adults in the same family can access shared child data. The inviting guardian is responsible for inviting only authorised people and removing access when it is no longer appropriate.

KidoSteps uses transparent, rule-based calculations to show trends, reminders and informational warnings. They do not diagnose a condition and do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects. They are not a substitute for a healthcare professional.

KidoSteps does not sell child data and does not use it for advertising or train third-party artificial-intelligence models.

8. Your and the child's data-protection rights

Subject to the conditions in applicable law, the adult user and the child (directly or through an authorised guardian) may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Consent can be withdrawn at any time. A request is normally answered without undue delay and within one month.

We may request information needed to verify identity and authority to act for a child. Requests are normally free of charge. Rights can be limited in specific cases required by law, and any refusal will be explained.

9. Security and incidents

KidoSteps uses HTTPS/TLS in transit, one-way password hashing, access controls based on authenticated family membership, restricted production configuration and secure storage for the app login token. No internet service is risk-free. Security measures are reviewed in proportion to the sensitivity of children's data.

If a personal-data breach is likely to create a high risk, affected people will be informed without undue delay as required by law.

10. Changes to this notice

Material changes will be communicated in the app or by email before they take effect where appropriate. A new consent will be requested if required. Previous versions and the change date will be retained. The product changelog is separate from this privacy notice.
