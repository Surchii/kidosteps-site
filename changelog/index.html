Product updates and material changes to KidoSteps.

This changelog highlights material updates that may affect features, functionality, or data handling.

Version 1.0.0

30 July 2026

  • Initial KidoSteps release.
  • Child profiles and shared family access for invited adults.
  • Feeding, sleep, diaper, growth, vaccination, milestone, note and planned-activity records.
  • Child photo gallery and profile images.
  • Local reminders, rule-based trends and informational warnings.
  • Local caching and offline support for selected flows, with synchronisation when available.
  • CSV/PDF export and optional Premium features.
  • In-app account deletion.

Planned improvements

Plans may change and do not form part of a paid subscription commitment.

  • Clearer trends and insights.
